Vincenzo Pasquadibisceglie's research sits at the intersection of **process mining**, **predictive process monitoring**, and **Data-Centric AI**, with applications ranging from business process management to healthcare. Below is a selection of research highlights.

### Concept drift & process discovery

- **Handling concept drifts with traditional process discovery algorithms** — *Journal of Intelligent Information Systems* 64(1), 179–213, 2025 (single-authored). Demonstrates independent scientific leadership on concept drift.
- **DARWIN: An online deep learning approach to handle concept drifts in predictive process monitoring** — *Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence* 123, 2023 (Q1).
- **PROMISE: Coupling predictive process mining to process discovery** — *Information Sciences* 606, 2022 (Q1), with Prof. Wil van der Aalst (RWTH Aachen), developed during a research stage at RWTH Aachen.

### Multimodal & explainable predictive process monitoring

- **Multimodal predictive process monitoring and its application to explainable clinical pathways** — *Information Systems* 139, 102698, with I. Donadello, A. Appice, O. Lanz, F.M. Maggi et al. Introduces a multimodal PPM framework applied to explainable clinical pathways.
- **PROPHET: Explainable Predictive Process Monitoring With Heterogeneous Graph Neural Networks** — *IEEE Transactions on Services Computing* 17(6), 2024 (Q1). Foundational architecture for interpretable PPM.
- **JARVIS: Joining Adversarial Training With Vision Transformers in Next-Activity Prediction** — *IEEE Transactions on Services Computing* 17(4), 2024 (Q1). Adversarial training for robust PPM representations.

### Data-Centric AI & language models

- **CICERONE: A natural language-based global approach for object-centric Predictive Process Monitoring** — *Information Systems*, 102788. Proposes a natural-language, object-centric reformulation of PPM.
- **Data-Centric AI Manifesto: How Data Quality Drives Modern AI** — *Electronics* 15(9), 1913, with D. Malerba, A. Poggi, M. Alviano et al. A community position paper defining the Data-Centric AI research agenda.
- **LUPIN: A LLM Approach for Activity Suffix Prediction in Business Process Event Logs** — ICPM 2024. Reuse of foundation LLMs for predictive process monitoring.

### Healthcare AI

- **Leveraging a Large Language Model to Predict Hospital Admissions of Emergency Department Patients** — *Expert Systems with Applications* 287, 2025 (Q1). Textual PPM applied to real hospital data.
