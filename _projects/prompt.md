## Overview

Predictive process monitoring is a branch of process mining focused on analyzing and improving business performance. The extensive availability of event logs from real-world scenarios (for example hospitals, banks and e-commerce) makes it possible to develop solutions that monitor how a process evolves. PROMPT (PRedictive mOnitoring with Multimodal Process daTa) focuses on the integration of multimodal logs, that is, data from different sources and of different types, such as event logs, sensor data and textual annotations, to provide new solutions for process prediction and optimization.

## Objective

The goal is to combine heterogeneous data to produce accurate insights into the dynamics of business processes. Traditionally, predictive models anticipate critical issues such as deviations, delays or operational anomalies using a single source of information. Multimodal event logs make it possible to include many more aspects of a process. For example, a multimodal event log in a hospital could enrich electronic patient records with examination images or vital-sign time series.

## Approach

A distinctive aspect of PROMPT is the use of multimodal neural networks and data fusion algorithms. They integrate structured logs (such as timestamps and case identifiers) with unstructured data (such as texts or images), using natural language processing and computer vision. The project aims at solutions applicable to several industrial sectors, including manufacturing, logistics and healthcare.

## Why high-performance computing

LLMs and Vision-Language Models (VLMs) show remarkable performance in many domains, but running them on a single GPU is a bottleneck in terms of efficiency, and some architectures (such as GPT-2) do not fit in the memory of a single GPU. A cluster of machines offers clear advantages in efficiency and performance, allowing PPM research to reach new milestones.
