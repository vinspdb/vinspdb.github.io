A seed project for an ERC Starting Grant

PROMETEO (PRedictive prOcess Monitoring in the Era of The data-cEntric AI wOrld) started as a seed project funded by the University of Bari Aldo Moro, with the specific purpose of laying the scientific groundwork for a proposal to the ERC Starting Grant call. The seed funding made it possible to build the first results, explore the research directions and consolidate the ideas that later became the ERC Starting Grant proposal.

Overview

The aim of PROMETEO is to address the open challenges of Data-Centric AI (DCAI) in the context of process mining, in order to extract the right value from process data.

Context: data as a driver

Data is crucial for economic growth, innovation, job creation and social development. The European Data Strategy and the White Paper on Artificial Intelligence are cornerstones of the Commission’s new digital strategy. Both put people at the centre of technology development and advocate protecting and advancing European values and rights in the development of AI within the real economy.

Data-Centric AI

From a research perspective, these challenges meet the new data-centric AI paradigm, which places data at the centre of AI projects. In most AI projects, the main goal is to learn a high-performing model from a predetermined dataset. However, high-performing model architectures are now readily available, while methods for improving datasets have lagged behind. The DCAI paradigm reverses the conventional approach: it asks researchers to improve the dataset while keeping the model fixed.

Poor data quality is pervasive across almost every industry, it has been estimated to cost the United States alone about $3 trillion per year, and managing it manually is a tremendous burden. We need automated methods and systematic engineering principles to guarantee the central role of data in AI projects.

Process mining

Process-Aware Information Systems (PAISs) are software systems that are an excellent source of raw data. With process mining techniques, it is possible to analyse this large amount of data to discover the underlying process model, to identify anomalies in particular process executions, or to improve the performance of the whole business.
